Five pages, written to be read in order and to stand without the raw notes.

- [Executive summary](executive-summary.md): the findings that carry the most
  weight, in one page.
- [Pipeline design](pipeline-design.md): the stages, their inputs, formulas,
  constants, outputs and validation cases.
- [Error budget](error-budget.md): every input ranked by its effect in seconds
  and metres, and the two product modes that follow from it.
- [Reading list and reference matrix](reading-list.md): the papers, code and
  datasets in the order a developer should take them.
- [Open questions](open-questions.md): the artefacts that would most change
  the conclusions.

Every claim here is carried from a raw note, and the citation travels with
it. The [raw research](../10-raw/_index.md) holds the full derivations.
