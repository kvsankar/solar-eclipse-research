Six pages, written to be read in order and to stand without the raw notes.

- [Executive summary](executive-summary.md): the findings that carry the most
  weight, in one page, for both cases and then for solar eclipses.
- [Solar eclipse pipeline](pipeline-design.md): the stages, their inputs,
  formulas, constants, outputs and validation cases.
- [Extending the pipeline to other events](other-events.md): what the solar
  pipeline shares with [lunar eclipses](../10-raw/30-lunar-eclipses/_index.md), occultations, transits and satellite
  phenomena, and what each one adds.
- [Solar eclipse error budget](error-budget.md): every input ranked by its
  effect in seconds and metres, and the two product modes that follow from it.
- [Reading list and reference matrix](reading-list.md): the papers, code and
  datasets for each research group, in the order a developer should take them.
- [Open questions](open-questions.md): the artefacts that would most change
  the conclusions, for each research group.

Every claim here is carried from a raw note, and the citation travels with
it. The [raw research](../10-raw/_index.md) holds the full derivations.
