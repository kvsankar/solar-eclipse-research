Every event on this site is one body hiding another from a third, and every
computation of one needs the same inputs. This group holds them:

- [Foundations](01-foundations/_index.md): the fundamental plane and the
  Besselian elements. The same construction serves solar eclipses, transits
  and occultations, with a shadow cone for the Sun and a cylinder for a star.
- [Earth model and time scales](07-earth-and-time/_index.md): the ellipsoid,
  terrain, ΔT, refraction, light-time and reference frames.
- [Lunar and solar model](08-lunar-and-solar-model/_index.md): the JPL
  ephemerides, the lunar radius ratio $k$, the centre of figure and libration.
- [Software and repositories](09-software-and-repos/_index.md): the
  production programs, open libraries, repositories and datasets.

The groups that follow apply these inputs to one kind of event each. Their
pages link back here rather than repeating them.
