A planet and its satellites produce every kind of event at once:
- **A satellite goes dark** when it enters the planet's shadow, or another
  satellite's. That is an eclipse, seen at the same instant by every observer.
- **A satellite disappears** behind the planet's disc, or crosses in front of
  it. Those are an occultation and a transit, which depend on the observer's
  viewpoint.
- **A satellite's shadow falls on the planet.** That is a shadow transit, an
  eclipse of a patch of cloud tops.

The topics here keep each planetary system together and label each event by
its kind.

- [Jupiter's satellites](15-jupiter-satellites/_index.md): the Galilean
  phenomena, the satellite theories and the [mutual events](15-jupiter-satellites/mutual-events.md).
- [Saturn and the outer satellites](16-saturn-and-outer-satellites/_index.md):
  ring geometry, satellite phenomena, and mutual events at Saturn, Uranus and
  [Pluto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pluto).
