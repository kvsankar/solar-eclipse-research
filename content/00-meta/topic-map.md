The map puts every topic of this site on one ring, grouped by section, with
the home page at the centre and the sections on the rings between. A curve
joins two topics when a page in one links to a page in the other, and a
thicker curve carries more links. Curves of one or two links are hidden
until the slider asks for them. Point at a topic, or move to it with the
Tab key, to light its links and dim the rest. Click a topic, or press
Enter on it, to keep it lit while you look, and click it again to open its
page. Esc clears it.

The table under the map lists the same topics with their pages and the
topics each one links to. The build draws both from the pages themselves,
so a new link appears here at the next build.
